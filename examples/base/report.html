Model basis

  • Historical 2024 disclosure anchors scale; 2025 remains a separate reconciliation review item.
  • Synthetic eligible capital equals equity: zero deductions and zero Tier 2 are assumptions, not independently verified eligibility.
  • All positions, sensitivities, credit exposures and scenario shocks are hypothetical; they do not represent the source institution's portfolio.
  • 10.5% is a configured total-capital monitoring target. No fundamental/basic capital buffer test or formal supervisory category is assigned.
  • One-year planning exercise with instantaneous shocks; no operating earnings, tax benefit, hedging response, retained profit or external capital injection.
  • Linear DV01 approximation; credit default proxy is separate from FX and rates books. Defaulted exposures remain in RWA; no relief assumed.
  • Credit migration and market/operational RWA multipliers are scenario overlays, not a reconstruction of CNBV regulatory schedules.
Public calibration source: issuer financial statements. Risk-weighted asset disclosures are explicitly unaudited. Independent project; no affiliation or regulatory submission. Output integrity is recorded in manifest.json.
